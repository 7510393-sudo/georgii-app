Graphomanus — Support

Write to support@graphomanus.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you did just before the problem.

Where are my entries?

Your entries are ordinary files in the folder you chose (Settings → Entries → Storage). Deleting the app does not delete them.

Restore my purchase

Settings → purchase section → “Restore purchases”.

Switching from another app

Settings → “Switch from another app” lists the apps we can import from.
